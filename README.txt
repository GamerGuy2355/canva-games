AFK SORTING - HTML5 V4

Open index.html in a modern desktop or mobile browser to play.
No account, installation, or internet connection is required.

NEW GAME: You begin with $0, 0 rebirths, and Bubble Sort unlocked.
SORTING: Bars automatically sort and earn money. SHUFFLE appears only when a sort is finished.
UPGRADES: Use MAX, X1, X5, and X10 to purchase from the right panel.
ALGORITHMS: Click the red book icon on the left. Scroll the card list using the mouse wheel, trackpad, touch swipe, scrollbar, or up/down buttons.
CHESTS: Timed chests spawn on the board. Click before their countdown expires. Five star positions appear, lighting up one by one. The number that lights up determines the rarity:
1 = Common, 2 = Uncommon, 3 = Rare, 4 = Legendary, 5 = Mythic.
STATS: Click the chart icon to see statistics or START NEW GAME to erase your HTML5 progress.

The game automatically saves locally in your browser. This V4 starts separately from previous prototypes.
